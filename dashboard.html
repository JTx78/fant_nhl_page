      Skaters score 2 per goal + 1 per assist (empty-net goals count fully for the skater). Each team pick scores its real goals against (excluding empty-net goals) as a deduction plus 10 per shutout, so that slot is usually negative — the standings table's EN figure is the raw empty-net-goal count against that team, shown for reference but not scored. Skater pts/GP is a roster's skater points divided by the games those skaters played; pts/night is total points divided by the game nights so far, so injuries and missed games count against it. A game night is any date on which a rostered player or team pick played. Hot & cold covers rostered skaters who played in the window.
